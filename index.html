Our Pastor

A shepherd for this season.

Pastor Caldwell came to Mt. Moriah believing that a church is more than a Sunday service. It is a place where people grow.

His ministry is built on a simple conviction: the gospel transforms whole lives. Not just souls, but minds, families, finances, and futures. He preaches Christ crucified and pursues a vision of community where grace is the ground, growth is the work, and gratitude is the response.

He is new to the Mount, but he is not new to the work. He arrives with a heart for people, a love for the Word, and a calling that this congregation has been preparing to receive.

We are not building a gathering. We are building a community of believers, growing together in every part of life.
