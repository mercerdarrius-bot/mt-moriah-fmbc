Our Story

More than a gathering.

Mt. Moriah has been a fixture in Quincy for generations. A place where families are raised, faith is built, and neighbors become one body.

Under Pastor Stephen L.M. Caldwell, we are entering a new chapter, rooted in the same faith that built this house and pointed toward the work still ahead.

Our Pastor

Pastor Caldwell preaches a gospel that reaches every part of a life. Not only the soul, but the mind, the family, and the future. He is new to the Mount, but he is not new to the work.

We are not building a gathering. We are building a community of believers, growing together in every part of life.
