Our Story

A continuation,
not a restart.

The story of Mt. Moriah is older than any one chapter. It belongs to the people who built this house, to the people who keep walking through its doors, and to the work still ahead.

The Arc
Est.
Quincy, FL

Mt. Moriah First Missionary Baptist Church has been a fixture in Quincy, Florida for generations. Across decades of change in the city, the South, and the country, this congregation has remained a place where people gather to worship, to learn, and to build a life together in faith.

Every congregation has seasons. There are seasons of growth, seasons of patience, and seasons of preparation. We have known each of them. What has never changed is the conviction that this church belongs to a story bigger than any one moment, and that the work of the gospel is never finished.

This is the chapter we have been preparing for.

What We Believe

Three words. One posture.

The shape of our faith, said simply. Each word leads to the next, and together they describe how we want to live.

i.

Grace

Unearned. Given freely.

We begin here because God begins here. Grace is not the reward at the end of effort. It is the ground we stand on before we have done anything at all. Every person who walks through these doors is met by it first.

ii.

Growth

Spirit, mind, and means.

Grace produces growth. We believe a believer should keep growing for the rest of their life, and that growth is not only spiritual. It includes how we think, how we care for our minds and bodies, how we handle our money, and how we build the futures we are responsible for.

iii.

Gratitude

The only honest response.

When you have received grace and you are walking in growth, gratitude is what comes out. Not as a feeling we perform, but as a posture we carry. We worship, we serve, and we give because we are grateful, not because we owe.

The Vision

A gospel that reaches every part of life.

We believe faith speaks to more than Sunday morning. So our ministry does, too.

01 · Spirit

Spiritual formation

Worship, scripture, prayer, and discipleship. The center of everything, and the reason the rest of this list matters.

02 · Mind

Mental and emotional health

Faith does not ask us to ignore what we feel. We hold space for grief, for anxiety, for the weight people carry, and we believe the church should walk with people through it.

03 · Means

Financial wellness

How we earn, save, give, and steward what we have been entrusted with. We teach this because the Bible teaches it, and because our families deserve to flourish.

04 · Community

Economic development

A healthy church helps build a healthy neighborhood. We invest in the people, the businesses, and the future of Quincy because this is where God has planted us.
