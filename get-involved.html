Wherever you are,
there is a step for you.

Faith moves one step at a time. Whether this is your first Sunday or your fortieth year at the Mount, there is a next thing God is asking of you, and a church ready to walk it with you.

Take Your Next Step

Four ways to begin.

Choose the one that fits where you are today. Tell us, and a pastor or team member follows up personally. No pressure, no crowd to get lost in.

    Ready to give your life to Jesus, or want to know what that means? Start here and we will help you take the first step.

    Baptism is a public declaration of a changed life. Let us know you are ready and we will prepare a date with you.

    Ready to call Mt. Moriah home? Membership is how you plant roots, belong, and grow with this church family.
